Vegetarian & Non-Vegetarian Menu

Freshly prepared food and serving arrangements for your event.

We prepare and serve a wide variety of vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes for weddings, receptions, birthdays, family functions and other occasions. Build a menu around your preferences, guest count and budget. Please confirm specific dishes with us when enquiring.

🌿 Vegetarian Menu

  • Veg Manchurian, Gobi 65 & Paneer Starters
  • Paneer Butter Masala & Kadai Paneer
  • Mixed Vegetable Curry & Dal Tadka
  • Veg Biryani, Veg Pulao & Steamed Rice
  • Sambar, Rasam & Curd Rice
  • Chapati, Poori, Naan & Parotta
  • Pani Puri, Chaat & Snack Counters
  • Fresh Salads, Pickles, Papad & Raita
  • Gulab Jamun, Payasam, Ice Cream & Desserts
  • Fruit Punch, Buttermilk & Welcome Drinks

🍗 Non-Vegetarian Menu

  • Chicken 65, Chicken Majestic & Chicken Fry
  • Chicken Tikka & Other Chicken Starters
  • Chicken Biryani & Chicken Dum Biryani
  • Mutton Biryani & Mutton Curry
  • Chicken Curry, Pepper Chicken & Kadai Chicken
  • Fish Fry & Fish Curry
  • Egg Masala & Egg Curry
  • Steamed Rice, Roti, Naan & Parotta
  • Salads, Raita, Mirchi Ka Salan & Gravies
  • Custom dishes on request, subject to confirmation

Menu items can be discussed and customised. Separate vegetarian and non-vegetarian preparation/serving arrangements can be discussed when you book.
